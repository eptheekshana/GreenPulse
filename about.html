About GreenPulse

GreenPulse is an open-source, edge-computing powered irrigation and monitoring system. It continuously reads environmental sensors and automatically controls water pumps based on precise soil saturation levels.


Hardware Components

  • ESP32 Microcontroller: The main processing unit providing dual-core performance.
  • DHT11 Sensor: Ambient temperature and relative humidity.
  • LDR Sensor: Photoresistor module tracking light intensity.
  • Soil & Water Sensors: Analog resistive sensors mapping precise saturation percentages.
  • Relay Module: Electrically isolates the low-voltage ESP32 from the high-power water pump circuit.
