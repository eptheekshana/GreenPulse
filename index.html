System Features

Automated Irrigation

Precise soil moisture tracking automatically controls water pumps to maintain perfect saturation, preventing over-watering.

Live Telemetry

High-resolution monitoring of temperature, humidity, and ambient light levels, streamed in real-time to your local dashboard.

Fail-Safe Protection

Integrated reservoir level sensing ensures water pumps are instantly disabled if the tank runs dry.

Local First

Operates entirely on your local Wi-Fi network. No cloud subscriptions, and no internet dependency required.

Hardware Components

ESP32 Microcontroller

The main processing unit providing dual-core performance and embedded Wi-Fi hosting capabilities.

DHT11 Sensor

Provides accurate ambient temperature and relative humidity readings for the climate matrix.

LDR Sensor

Photoresistor module tracking light intensity to determine optimal growing conditions.

Soil & Water Sensors

Analog resistive sensors providing raw voltage feedback mapped to precise saturation percentages.

Relay Module

Electrically isolates the low-voltage ESP32 from the high-power water pump circuit.

Execution Logic

1

Sensor Polling

Reads raw analog and digital data every 2000ms.

2

Threshold Analysis

Compares readings against stored user thresholds.

3

Action Execution

Relay triggers pump if soil is dry AND water is available.
