Automate your greenhouse with GreenPulse

A highly reliable, edge-computing powered irrigation and monitoring system. GreenPulse continuously tracks environmental sensors and intelligently controls water pumps based on precise soil saturation levels.

Next-Gen Automation

Experience seamless integration of hardware and software designed for the perfect harvest.

Automated Irrigation

Precise soil moisture tracking automatically controls water pumps to maintain perfect saturation, preventing both under-watering and over-watering. Your plants get exactly what they need, exactly when they need it.

Live Telemetry

High-resolution monitoring of temperature, humidity, and ambient light levels. All metrics are streamed in real-time to your beautifully designed local dashboard with zero latency.

Fail-Safe Protection

Integrated reservoir level sensing ensures water pumps are instantly disabled if the tank runs dry, preventing hardware damage.

Local First Edge AI

Operates entirely on your local network. No cloud subscriptions, no data harvesting, and completely independent of internet connectivity.

Industrial-Grade Hardware

Built with robust components to ensure 24/7 reliability in demanding environments.

ESP32 Edge Microcontroller

The main processing unit providing dual-core performance and embedded Wi-Fi hosting capabilities for the entire system ecosystem.

Climate & Light Matrix

Integrated DHT11 and LDR sensors provide highly accurate ambient temperature, relative humidity, and light intensity readings.

Soil & Reservoir Probes

Analog resistive sensors provide raw voltage feedback that is mapped to precise saturation and volume percentages in real-time.

Intelligent Execution Loop

The core logic engine running continuously on the edge.

1. Polling Phase

Reads raw analog and digital data every 2000ms.

2. Analysis Phase

Compares real-time readings against predefined optimal growth thresholds.

3. Execution Phase

Solid-state relays trigger irrigation if soil is dry and water reserves are sufficient.
